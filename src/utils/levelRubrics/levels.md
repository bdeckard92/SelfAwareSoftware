🧱 Rubric Dimensions (Use Across All Levels)

These are your scoring axes:

Execution (Can they deliver?)
Ownership (Do they take responsibility?)
Collaboration (How they work with others)
Technical Judgment (How they think)
Influence (How they impact others)
🧑‍💻 Software Engineer 1 (SE1)
🧠 Profile

Learning to contribute independently with guidance.

📊 Rubric

Execution

Completes well-defined tasks
Needs guidance for ambiguity

Ownership

Owns small tasks
Escalates blockers quickly

Collaboration

Participates when asked
Benefits from pairing and feedback

Technical Judgment

Follows established patterns
Focuses on “how” more than “why”

Influence

Limited to own work
Learns from others
🎯 Signals for Game Answers
“I ask for help when stuck”
“I follow existing patterns”
“I rely on code reviews to improve”
👨‍💻 Software Engineer 2 (SE2)
🧠 Profile

Solid independent contributor with growing judgment.

📊 Rubric

Execution

Delivers features end-to-end
Handles moderate ambiguity

Ownership

Owns features (not just tasks)
Anticipates common issues

Collaboration

Actively participates in pairing/reviews
Starts helping others

Technical Judgment

Understands tradeoffs (basic level)
Makes reasonable decisions

Influence

Impacts immediate team
Shares knowledge occasionally
🎯 Signals for Game Answers
“I can take a feature from idea to delivery”
“I consider tradeoffs”
“I help teammates when I can”
🧠 Senior Software Engineer
🧠 Profile

Drives solutions, mentors others, improves team effectiveness.

📊 Rubric

Execution

Leads complex projects
Breaks down ambiguity

Ownership

Owns systems or major components
Proactively identifies problems

Collaboration

Mentors others
Improves team practices (pairing, reviews, retros)

Technical Judgment

Strong tradeoff analysis
Designs maintainable systems

Influence

Shapes team decisions
Raises engineering standards
🎯 Signals for Game Answers
“I help others grow”
“I improve how the team works”
“I think about long-term maintainability”
🧭 Staff Software Engineer
🧠 Profile

Multiplies impact across teams through strategy and influence.

📊 Rubric

Execution

Drives cross-team initiatives
Aligns technical direction

Ownership

Owns problems, not just systems
Focuses on business + technical outcomes

Collaboration

Enables teams, not just individuals
Builds alignment across groups

Technical Judgment

System-level thinking
Balances short vs long-term across org

Influence

Influences without authority
Sets direction and standards
🎯 Signals for Game Answers
“I align teams around shared solutions”
“I focus on broader impact, not just my team”
“I help others make better decisions”
